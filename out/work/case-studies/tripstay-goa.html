TripStay Goa

A full property stay, documented — check-in to closing frame.

01The Brief

TripStay brought this work on to document a full stay at their Goa property — content built to cover the property itself, room by room, rather than a single highlight reel.

The brief covered check-in through checkout: bedrooms, living areas, kitchen, bathrooms, balcony views, the pool, amenities and the surrounding neighbourhood — shot to be genuinely usable across TripStay's own Instagram, Facebook, website, ads, brochures and WhatsApp marketing.

Location
Goa, India
Property Coverage
Full stay, room by room
Delivery
Google Drive
02Creative Direction

Rather than a single polished reel, the direction called for full coverage — a cinematic walkthrough of every room, lifestyle shots of the amenities and surroundings, and both a morning and evening read of the same spaces.

Video was shot in LOG rather than a baked-in look, so the footage could carry a considered cinematic grade in the final edit and still remain flexible for TripStay's own future use.

03The Story

The film and photo set follow one genuine stay through the property — check-in, the rooms, the pool, the view, the ambiance of morning and evening — built to read as a real stay rather than a listing shoot.

04Production

Shot and edited solo on location, built around full coverage of the property rather than a single hero angle — interiors, the pool, the view, and the property's morning and evening character.

Raw video was recorded in LOG. Custom LUTs built for the final cinematic grade were delivered alongside the raw footage, so TripStay can re-cut the material themselves.

Crew
Solo — direction, camera, edit
Camera
Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra
Video Format
4K, LOG — vertical & horizontal
Edit
Adobe Premiere Pro
05Deliverables

What this collaboration actually produced.

  • 1 edited Instagram Reel — full stay experience
  • Instagram Story files from the stay
  • Edited property photos
  • Raw, unedited photos
  • 15–20 raw video clips, captured in LOG, including both vertical and horizontal footage
  • LOG → final colour grading LUTs, delivered for TripStay's own use
