Giri Mane Homestay

A homestay, documented as a place — not a listing.

01The Brief

Giri Mane Homestay is a documentation and storytelling collaboration — the property captured as somewhere worth staying, shot and edited with the same standard as every other piece of this work.

Content was built for the homestay's own use across Airbnb and Google — photography and two short-form Reels covering the property and its setting.

02Creative Direction

A homestay asks for a different eye than a hotel. The story isn't in a lobby or a brand standard — it's in the specific, unrepeatable details a guest actually notices about a place that isn't a chain.

Two Reels carried two different registers: one a static, observational look at the property itself, the other a talking, cinematic piece giving the homestay a voice.

03The Story

Set among tea and coffee plantation country, the property is framed as a slow-travel stay — its red-brick veranda, surrounding tree line and quiet pace treated as the point, not backdrop filler between amenity shots.

04Production

Shot and edited solo on location, producing a full photo set of the property plus two distinct Reel formats built for Airbnb and Google promotion.
